I’m Sergi Mansilla, an Engineering Leader focused on bringing AI and computing to the edge. I spend most of my time trying to understand how people and technology can work together.

I’m Director of Engineering at Edge Impulse, part of Qualcomm Technologies, where I lead engineering teams building tools that bring AI closer to the physical world.


### My Path
Over the years I’ve wandered through many corners of the industry:
- Building and scaling the [Edge Impulse Physical AI platform](https://www.edgeimpulse.com/) as it grew into a community of 350,000 people doing incredible things with tiny devices.
- Leading engineering teams at [Nearform](https://nearform.com/).
- Scaling the platform team at Spanish unicorn [Glovo](https://glovoapp.com/) during hypergrowth.
- Working with [Mozilla](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/) on [Firefox OS](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Firefox_OS).
- Designing systems at [Telenor Digital](https://www.telenor.com/).
- Building [Cloud9 IDE](https://aws.amazon.com/cloud9/) (now part of AWS).
- Leading the interface framework team at [TomTom](https://www.tomtom.com/).
- Co-founding [JustPeace Labs](https://justpeacelabs.org/), a non-profit focused on ethics and human rights in technology.

None of these steps were planned in advance; each one taught me something new about how people build, collaborate, and imagine the future.

I also write or speak about topics like AI at the edge, reactive systems, and the sometimes messy process of building things that last. I also wrote the book [*Reactive Programming with RxJS*](https://pragprog.com/titles/smreactjs5/reactive-programming-with-rxjs-5/), which grew out of a desire to explain patterns I found beautiful.
